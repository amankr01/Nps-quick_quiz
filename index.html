How to Play ?¿

Welcome to Quick Quiz! 🎯 The purpose of this game is to build your financial literacy about the National Pension Scheme (NPS) — a smart way to secure your future and prepare for retirement.

👉 To start, press the big yellow PLAY button located at the bottom-right corner of the screen. Each round will show you one question at a time. - Select the option you think is correct. - If your answer is ✅ correct, you’ll move to the next question. - If ❌ wrong, you’ll see the right answer immediately.

At the end of the game, your results will be saved and displayed under the Past Game Data section so you can track your progress over time.

Keep playing, keep learning — and step closer to financial independence with every round. 💡
